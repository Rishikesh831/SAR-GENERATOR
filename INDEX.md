## 🚀 Quick Start (60 seconds)

1. **Look in navbar** (top-right):
   - Find Sun ☀️ or Moon 🌙 icon
   - It's between "Regulatory Compliant" badge and your profile

2. **Click the icon**:
   - Theme changes instantly
   - Colors switch from light ↔ dark

3. **Close the app**:
   - Reopen later
   - Theme remembered! ✨

**That's it! You're done. 🎉**

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Where's the toggle button?**
A: Top navbar between "Regulatory Compliant" badge and your profile. Look for ☀️ or 🌙 icon.

**Q: Which doc should I read first?**
A: QUICK_START_GUIDE.md for everyone!

**Q: Is it safe to use?**
A: 100% safe. Zero breaking changes. No backend impact.

**Q: Will my preference be saved?**
A: Yes! In browser localStorage.

**Q: Can I customize the colors?**
A: Yes! Edit `.light` variables in `index.css`.
